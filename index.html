Privacy Policy for ModelNest

Effective Date: Nov 23, 2026  |  Last Updated: Nov 23, 2026
Developer: Izhar Tech Sol  |  App Name: ModelNest
Key Privacy Summary: ModelNest does not collect, store, track, or sell your personal information or chat history on developer servers. Your OpenRouter API key is stored securely on your device using encrypted local storage. Chat requests and media attachments are sent directly from your device to the OpenRouter API over encrypted HTTPS connection.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ModelNest ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Izhar Tech Sol. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed through your use of the ModelNest mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and use ModelNest. ModelNest is a client interface that connects to third-party artificial intelligence (AI) models via the OpenRouter API (https://openrouter.ai).

By using ModelNest, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Key Privacy Principles

  • No Server-Side Personal Data Collection: We do not operate developer servers to collect, store, track, or sell your personal information, name, email address, phone number, or chat history.
  • Local Storage & On-Device Security: Your OpenRouter API key is stored securely on your device using encrypted storage (`flutter_secure_storage`). Your chat history and preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • Direct Communication with OpenRouter: Your queries, prompts, and attachments are transmitted directly from your device to the OpenRouter API over an encrypted HTTPS connection to generate AI responses.
  • Full Data Ownership & Control: You can delete your chat history, remove your API key, or erase all app data at any time directly within the App or through your device settings.

3. Information We Process and How It Is Used

A. OpenRouter API Key

When you enter your OpenRouter API Key into ModelNest, it is stored locally on your device using platform-native encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android). The API key is used exclusively to authenticate your API requests directly with OpenRouter. Your API key is never sent to Izhar Tech Sol servers or shared with any unauthorized third party.

B. Chat Conversations and Prompts

Text prompts, user messages, selected AI models, and response history are stored locally on your device's internal storage to display your chat history. When you send a message, it is transmitted over secure HTTPS directly to OpenRouter's API endpoints (openrouter.ai) to generate responses. We do not inspect, log, monitor, or sell your chat content.

C. Camera, Photos, and Media Files (Attachments)

ModelNest allows you to attach images or documents to your AI chat prompts:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Accessed only when you explicitly tap the camera option inside the App to take a photo to attach to an AI prompt.
  • Photo & File Access: Accessed only when you explicitly select photos or files from your device gallery/file picker to attach to an AI prompt.

Attached media files are processed locally on your device and transmitted directly to OpenRouter API along with your prompt. They are never accessed in the background or collected by Izhar Tech Sol.

4. Android Device Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose Requirement
android.permission.CAMERA Allows you to capture photos directly inside the App to attach to AI model prompts. Optional (Runtime prompt on demand)
android.permission.INTERNET Required to send prompts to OpenRouter API endpoints and fetch available AI models. Mandatory (Network calls)
Photo / File Picker Allows you to select existing images or documents from your device to attach to AI prompts. Optional (User-triggered picker)

5. Third-Party Services & API Providers

ModelNest integrates with third-party service providers to deliver AI features and application updates. Data transmitted to these services is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • OpenRouter API (https://openrouter.ai): Provides AI model access and prompt processing.
    Privacy Policy: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
  • Google Play Services / In-App Updates: Facilitates secure application updates via Google Play Store.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Security Practices

We implement robust technical safeguards to protect your information:

  • Encrypted API Key Storage: API keys are secured on-device using Android KeyStore & EncryptedSharedPreferences via flutter_secure_storage.
  • Transport Encryption (TLS/HTTPS): All network communications with OpenRouter API endpoints use industry-standard HTTPS encryption.
  • Local Sandboxing: Chat history and local settings are stored strictly within the App's isolated sandbox on your device.

7. Data Retention & Data Deletion

All data resides locally on your device. You have full control to clear or delete your data at any time:

  • Delete Chat History: Delete individual chats or clear history directly within the App.
  • Remove API Key: Remove or change your OpenRouter API key in the App Settings.
  • Complete Reset: Clear app storage via Android Settings > Apps > ModelNest > Storage > Clear Storage, or uninstall the App.

8. Children's Privacy

ModelNest is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at itsolution20023@gmail.com.
